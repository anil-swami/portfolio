Heat 01: Where the plant floor meets the public feed

The brief

Heavy industry does real work in its communities, but that work rarely makes it past the factory gate. The stories sit with people on the ground; the decisions about what to say sit somewhere else.

The approach

Work in both rooms. Plan, shoot, edit and publish CSR social content end to end, while taking part in the operational and business decisions behind it. What gets said is shaped by how the plant really runs, and what gets decided accounts for how it will land in public.

Outcomes

  • A steady, recognisable CSR voice, sustained for more than five years
  • In-house production from site shoot to published post, with no agency hand-off
  • Communication weighed at the decision table, not bolted on after it

Heat 02: A new company, online from day one

The brief

A newly acquired steel company needed a web presence of its own: credible to buyers and partners, distinct in its own right, yet clearly part of the wider group.

The approach

Design and build the whole site in-house: structure, content hierarchy, visual design and a hand-written front end. Responsive from the first sketch, light enough to load quickly on mobile networks, and simple for the team to keep up to date.

Outcomes

  • A complete corporate website, designed and developed end to end
  • An identity that stands on its own and still sits comfortably in the group family
  • No agency dependency for updates or new pages

Heat 03: Reports in. Insight out.

The brief

Production and business reports arrive faster than anyone can read them properly. The numbers are all there. The time to find what actually changed isn't.

The approach

Small internal web apps with a three-step flow. Upload a report as it comes out of existing systems. Analyse it with AI that structures the data, compares it with earlier periods and flags anything unusual. Get insights as a plain-language summary, plus the questions worth asking next.

Outcomes

  • A first read of a routine report in minutes, not hours
  • The same summary structure every time, so periods compare at a glance
  • Tools shaped by someone who reads these reports, not a generic dashboard

Heat 04: A new cause, finding its voice

The brief

A newly launched NGO with a clear mission and no audience yet. No content library, no production team and no budget to waste.

The approach

Run its social channels from the first post: tone of voice, formats, calendar and community. Produce short-form video with AI-assisted steps for the slow parts (rough cuts, captions, clean-up, versions), then finish every piece by hand so it moves fast without feeling machine-made.

Outcomes

  • A social presence built from zero
  • A repeatable video workflow sized for a small team
  • AI for speed, human editing for care

Heat 05: Campaigns that work on a wall and in a feed

The brief

Development messages have to land with very different audiences: on a printed poster in a community space, and on a phone screen in a scrolling feed.

The approach

Design campaigns as systems rather than one-offs. One core idea, adapted across posters, social creatives and print collateral, in every size and format needed, without losing clarity or consistency.

Outcomes

  • Campaign sets that stay consistent from print to digital
  • Print-ready artwork and platform-ready social formats from one design
  • Clear messages first, decoration second

§ 03 — The elements

Six elements, one practice

Each one stands on its own. Together they make work that no single discipline would get to alone.

  1. 01

    Op

    Operations & decisions

    Production-side know-how and a seat at the table for business decisions. Knowing how the plant actually runs is what makes the communication and the tools credible.

    Production reporting · CSR programmes · Planning · Cross-team decisions

  2. 02

    Ai

    AI tools

    Internal web apps that use AI to read reports, structure them and surface what changed. Small, specific and quick to put to work.

    LLM APIs · Prompt design · Document parsing · AI-assisted editing

  3. 03

    Wd

    Web development

    Full sites and web apps, designed and hand-built. Responsive, accessible and quick to load, with no agency in the loop.

    HTML · CSS · JavaScript · Responsive design · Accessibility

  4. 04

    Sm

    Social media

    Strategy, calendars, copy and community for brands and causes, from an industrial group's CSR to an NGO's first posts.

    Short-form video that is shot, cut and captioned, with AI-assisted steps for speed and hand-editing for feel.

    Posters · Campaign systems · Print production · Social creatives
